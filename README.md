Title:

Modeling EV Charging Access and Urban Adoption Patterns with Probabilistic Graphical Models

Abstract:

This project will use probabilistic graphical models to study how social, economic, geographic, and infrastructure factors relate to electric vehicle charging access and adoption patterns. Variables such as income, population density, urbanization, charging station availability, and EV registration levels will be represented in a Bayesian network to model probabilistic dependencies among them. The goal is to identify which factors are most strongly associated with uneven EV infrastructure distribution and to explore how changes in one factor may affect the likelihood of broader EV adoption.
