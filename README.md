Title:

Modeling Inequities in EV Charging Access with Probabilistic Graphical Models

Abstract:

This project will use probabilistic graphical models to study patterns of electric vehicle charging access across different communities. The project will model relationships among socioeconomic, geographic, demographic, and infrastructure variables such as income, population density, urbanization, EV registration levels, and charging station availability. A Bayesian network or latent variable model will be used to represent probabilistic dependencies among these factors and to identify different community profiles related to EV infrastructure access. The goal is to understand how observed community characteristics are associated with uneven EV charging availability and to explore how probabilistic graphical models can help explain variation in EV adoption environments.
